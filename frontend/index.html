Case #1: The Midnight Ledger

DIFFICULTY: EASY TYPE: THEFT DATE: 2026-09-10
A critical financial ledger containing proprietary corporate accounting records has vanished from a secured executive office. The primary financial director was found injured, and multiple corporate executives claim inconsistent alibis.

Mandatory Investigative Objectives:

  • Query Access Logs: Determine who accessed Floor 7 and the Executive Office between 20:30 and 21:40.
  • Inspect Evidence: Cross-reference fingerprints, security cards, and discarded items recovered on scene.
  • Verify Alibis: Interrogate suspects with contradictory phone and transaction histories.
  • Execute Warrant: Identify the guilty suspect and substantiate the motive and method beyond reasonable doubt.

🔍 2. Database Tables You Can Query

In Search Tables, write standard SELECT statements. Available tables:

Table Name Columns What to Look For
suspects name, occupation, motive, alibi What each person claims they were doing
access_logs location, access_time, person_id Keycard swipe times (proves who was actually there)
phone_records caller, receiver, call_time Call times that disprove dinner or alibi claims
evidence evidence_name, found_at, description Fingerprints, weapons, and forensics

⚡ 3. 1-Click Starter Queries

Click any button below to immediately paste the query into your active Search tab:

SELECT * FROM suspects WHERE case_id = {CASE_ID};
SELECT * FROM access_logs WHERE case_id = {CASE_ID};
SELECT * FROM phone_records WHERE case_id = {CASE_ID};
SELECT * FROM evidence WHERE case_id = {CASE_ID};

🎯 4. Step-by-Step Walkthrough to Win

  1. Step 1: Read the victim and crime report in Clues. Note the time of death or crime.
  2. Step 2: Check each suspect's Alibi in Suspects (e.g. "Claims they were at home at 21:00").
  3. Step 3: Query access_logs or phone_records for that time. Look for someone who lied about their whereabouts!
  4. Step 4: Open Arrest, select the suspect, explain why their alibi fails, and click Submit Arrest Warrant.
